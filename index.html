Services

Four ways we work

Each engagement stands on its own. Most clients start with one and add the next once the first is holding.

Microsoft 365

Tenant configuration and knowledge organization — SharePoint, Teams, Exchange, and OneDrive set up the way your people actually work rather than the way the defaults assume.

  • Site architecture and permissions
  • Knowledge organization and findability
  • Governance and lifecycle policy

AI Enablement

Copilot readiness and rollout: licensing, permissions hygiene, agent design, and the guardrails that make AI output something you can put in front of a client.

  • Copilot readiness assessment
  • Oversharing and permissions remediation
  • Custom agents and prompt libraries

Process Optimization

Map what actually happens today, remove the handoffs that don't earn their keep, and automate what's left with the Power Platform.

  • Process mapping and bottleneck analysis
  • Power Automate workflows
  • Forms, approvals, and reporting

Workforce Development

Role-based training that lands — short sessions built on your real documents, with reference material your team still reaches for once we're gone.

  • Role-based M365 and Copilot training
  • Live sessions and recorded reference
  • Written runbooks and quick guides
